Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about orders, sizing, care and payment. Cannot find yours? We are one message away.

How long will my order take to arrive?

Express delivery takes 1 to 3 business days and standard delivery 3 to 6 business days from dispatch. Made-to-measure pieces need a little longer. See Shipping & Returns for the full table.

Is delivery free?

Express delivery is complimentary on orders over PKR 25,000. Below that, express costs PKR 800 and standard costs PKR 400. Collecting from our salon is always complimentary.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Please write to our concierge as soon as possible with your order number. If your piece has not yet been cut or altered, we will happily make the change.

Do you deliver outside Pakistan?

We currently deliver within Pakistan only. Write to us if you are abroad and we will see what we can arrange.

How do I choose my abaya size?

Abayas are sized by length (52", 54", 56", 58"). Measure from your shoulder to the floor, or use our height guide. Between two lengths, take the longer one.

What if the length is not right?

Every piece includes complimentary alterations to length. Enter your height at checkout or book a fitting, and our atelier will adjust it by hand.

Do your pieces run large or small?

Our abayas are cut relaxed and our co-ords and dresses are tailored with ease. Each product page describes its fit, and our concierge will gladly advise on a specific piece.

How should I clean silk?

Dry clean structured or lined silk. Unlined silk can be hand washed cool with a pH-neutral detergent. Always check the care label first. Our care guide has the details.

How do I remove creases?

Steam is the gentlest option for every fabric we use. For silk, steam from the inside or iron on the reverse on low heat with a cloth between.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Cash on delivery, direct bank transfer, and Visa, Mastercard and UnionPay cards.

Is cash on delivery available everywhere?

It is available across Pakistan. For larger orders we may call to confirm, or ask for a small advance, before preparing your piece.

What is your return policy?

You can request an exchange or return within the period stated on our returns page, provided the piece is unworn with its tags attached. Altered pieces can be exchanged only if faulty.

Will I get a tracking number?

Yes. We send tracking details as soon as your order is dispatched.
