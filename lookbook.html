I
Chapter One

Morning Light

The first hour belongs to you, before the day has asked for anything.

Sand crepe and raw linen in the soft gold of a Lahore sunrise. Separates that move easily from breakfast to the boardroom, and never look as though they tried.

II
Chapter Two

The Gallery Hour

Structure is a kind of silence. The best rooms are built from it.

Wool cut like architecture and cashmere that falls in a single line. Pieces for the afternoon you spend among beautiful things and quiet people.

III
Chapter Three

Evening Drape

Silk does not announce itself. It arrives a moment after you do.

Hand-pleated columns, tiered habotai and a raw silk abaya the colour of the hour after dusk. For dinners that run long and rooms that go quiet when you enter.

IV
Chapter Four

Stillness

Nothing added. Nothing missing.
