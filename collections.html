The fabric library

Four materials carry every collection. Here is how each one feels, behaves and should be cared for.

Mulberry Silk

Feels
Cool, fluid, with a soft sheen
Wear
Evenings, occasions, all year
Care

Belgian Linen

Feels

Wear
