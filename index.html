From Sketch To Silhouette

Slowly made, never rushed

Every SENORA piece passes through four pairs of hands before it reaches yours. It takes weeks, and we would not have it any other way.

  1. I

    Drape & Draw

    Each form begins on the mannequin, pinned and re-pinned until the line falls without effort.

  2. II

    Source

    Mulberry silk, virgin wool and Belgian linen, chosen for weight, hand and the way they age.

  3. III

    Cut & Construct

    Zero-waste patterns and concealed seams, built to hold their shape through years of wear.

  4. IV

    Finish By Hand

    Hems turned, buttons set and every piece pressed and wrapped in our Lahore atelier.

OUR MANIFESTO

“Modesty is not about hiding—it is an intentional architecture of grace, quiet confidence, and sculptural poise.”

Founded on the belief that modesty should never compromise contemporary artistry, SENORA creates garments that honor presence rather than distraction. We eliminate ornamental excess in favor of purposeful drape, heavyweight natural fabrics, and uncompromising standards of finish.

Every seam is considered. Every silhouette is tested for movement, dignity, and longevity in our Lahore atelier—ensuring you feel completely at ease from boardroom meetings to gallery openings.

Ethical Sourcing

Cruelty-free peace silks, certified organic wool blends, and biodegradable linings.

Made to Measure

Every piece can be customized to your exact height and sleeve specifications at no extra charge.
