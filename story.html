A Letter From The Founder

I wanted clothes that asked nothing of me.

I started SENORA because I could not find what I was looking for: modest clothing that was beautiful first, and modest second. Pieces with weight and line and honest fabric, made by people who cared about the hem as much as the silhouette.

So we began with a table, a mannequin and a few metres of silk. We cut slowly. We threw away more than we kept. Every piece you see here survived that process, and we would not send anything out that we would not wear ourselves.

The Manifesto

Our principles

Fewer things.

We release small capsules, not endless collections. If a piece is not needed, it is not made.

Made slowly.

Each garment passes through four pairs of hands. It takes weeks, and we would not have it any other way.

Cut to last.

Concealed seams, honest fabrics and patterns built to hold their shape for years, not seasons.

Worn with intention.

Modesty is not hiding. It is an intentional architecture of grace, quiet confidence and sculptural poise.

Inside the atelier

Pins, chalk, steam and patience. This is where every SENORA piece takes its shape.
