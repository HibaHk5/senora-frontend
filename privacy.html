What we collect

When you place an order, request an appointment or join our list, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, delivery address and order details. We do not store card numbers.

How we use it

  • To prepare, deliver and support your order.
  • To respond to concierge and appointment requests.
  • To send news of new collections, only if you have asked us to.

What stays in your browser

To keep your shopping bag and wishlist as you move between pages, this site saves them in your browser's local storage on your own device. It contains product choices only, never your name, address or payment details. You can clear it at any time from your browser settings.
