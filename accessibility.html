Our commitment

We want everyone to be able to browse and buy from SENORA, whether they use a keyboard, a screen reader, a phone or a large display. We are working to improve the accessibility of this site.

What we have built in

  • A skip link at the top of every page, and full keyboard navigation.
  • Visible focus states on interactive elements.
  • Descriptive text for images, and labelled form fields with clear error messages.
  • Layouts that adapt from small phones to large screens.
  • Reduced motion: animations are switched off for visitors who prefer it in their system settings.
